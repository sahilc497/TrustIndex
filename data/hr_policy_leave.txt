LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY

Section 1: Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 annual paid leaves per calendar year. Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year for a maximum of 1 year. Annual leave must be requested at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal. Employees who resign are paid out for unused annual leave at their final gross salary rate.

Section 2: Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leaves per calendar year. Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate submitted to HR within 5 working days of returning to work. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and unused sick leave is not paid out upon resignation.

Section 3: Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Maternity leave must be applied for at least 8 weeks before the expected delivery date. Adoption leave of 12 weeks is available to employees who legally adopt a child under the age of 1.

Section 4: Probation Period Leave
Employees on probation (first 90 days of employment) are entitled to 5 paid leaves only. Full leave entitlements apply once probation is successfully completed and confirmed in writing by the reporting manager.
