WORK FROM HOME AND REMOTE WORK POLICY

Section 1: Eligibility
Employees who have completed 6 months of continuous employment are eligible to apply for work-from-home arrangements. Employees still on probation are not eligible for work from home under any circumstances. Approval is granted by the direct reporting manager and must be renewed every 6 months.

Section 2: Frequency
Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Employees in customer-facing roles may work from home up to 1 day per week only. Full-time remote work (5 days per week) requires written approval from the department head and HR director jointly.

Section 3: Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office setup allowance of 15,000 INR for employees approved for permanent remote work. This allowance is not provided for employees on the standard 2-day hybrid arrangement.

Section 4: Core Hours
All employees, whether working from home or office, must be available online between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST, referred to as core hours. Meetings scheduled outside core hours require 24 hours advance notice.
