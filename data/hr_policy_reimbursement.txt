EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Section 1: Travel Reimbursement
Employees traveling for official business are reimbursed for economy class airfare, train fare, and pre-approved local transport. Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be processed except with written approval from the finance director.

Section 2: Meal Allowance
Employees on official travel receive a daily meal allowance of 1,200 INR within India and 40 USD for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Section 3: Internet and Phone Reimbursement
Employees approved for permanent remote work receive a monthly internet reimbursement of 1,000 INR. This reimbursement is not available to employees on the standard hybrid work arrangement. Phone bill reimbursement of up to 500 INR per month is available to employees in sales and client-facing roles only.

Section 4: Approval Workflow
All reimbursement claims above 5,000 INR require approval from both the reporting manager and the finance department. Claims below 5,000 INR require only reporting manager approval.
